Matin Darwish (born 1 March) is a British software engineer, student entrepreneur and content creator from Leeds, England, of Kurdish (Sorani) heritage. At the age of 18 he founded the AI automation agency AutoFlow Leeds, which trades under his sole-trader business Darwish Enterprises. He is known for his software and hardware engineering projects and for documenting his work as a UK A-level student "building in public" on TikTok as @matindarw.

Darwish works as a software engineer across full-stack web, embedded systems and artificial-intelligence development. His best-known projects include CalcOS, an open-source graphing-calculator operating system, and Aether IDE, an AI-first desktop code editor. Alongside his studies he is pursuing a career in aerospace engineering.

Early life and education [edit]

Matin Darwish was born on 1 March in Leeds, England, into a family of Kurdish (Sorani) heritage, and grew up in the city.[1] He attended The Ruth Gorse Academy in Leeds before continuing to sixth form at Roundhay School, where he studied A-levels in Physics, Mathematics and Computer Science.

Darwish has set his sights on a career in aerospace engineering, researching degree apprenticeships with companies including Airbus, BAE Systems, Rolls-Royce and GE Aerospace.

Career [edit]

Darwish works as a software engineer, building across full-stack web, embedded systems and artificial-intelligence applications, and describes his hands-on, AI-assisted approach to development as "vibecoding". His primary development platform is the Fedora Linux operating system.[2]

AutoFlow Leeds and Darwish Enterprises

In May 2026, at the age of 18, Darwish founded AutoFlow Leeds, an AI automation agency that builds custom workflow automations for small businesses in the Leeds area, such as restaurants, salons, gyms and accountancies. The agency operates as a brand of Darwish Enterprises, his registered sole-trader business.[3]

AutoFlow Leeds offers productised services — built in TypeScript or n8n and powered by the Claude AI platform — on a fixed-price model, with a "code you own, no lock-in" approach and fast delivery. Its public-facing site is autoflowleeds.co.uk.

Notable projects [edit]

CalcOS

CalcOS is an open-source graphing-calculator operating system that runs on the ESP32-S3 microcontroller. Built around the LVGL graphics library, it includes a scientific calculator, a 2D function grapher, matrix tools, an equation solver and an integrated AI tutor powered by a remote large-language-model API. The project was developed as Darwish's A-level Computer Science non-exam assessment.[4]

Aether IDE

Aether IDE is an AI-first desktop integrated development environment built with Tauri, Rust and React. It features a real terminal, deep AI integration and an autonomous tool-using agent designed to assist developers directly inside the editor.

Termia

Termia is a Termux-inspired shell environment for the NumWorks graphing calculator, paired with a Rust bridge and a Tauri-based desktop companion, allowing more advanced interaction with the device over USB.

Other projects

  • NexaCare — an AI-powered WhatsApp receptionist designed for clinics.
  • ESP32-S3 travel router — a compact custom networking device with NAT and custom DNS.
  • OpenJarvis — contributor to the local-first AI agent framework, including work on NPU acceleration.

Content creation [edit]

Darwish creates short-form video content on TikTok under the handle @matindarw, where his work centres on the theme of a UK A-level student "building in public" — sharing his engineering projects, startup progress and study journey. He is also active on Instagram and produces Islamic da'wah-style content using AI design tools.[1]

Personal life [edit]

Darwish lives in Leeds, England, and is Muslim. Outside of engineering, his interests include fashion, gaming and Linux and open-source technology culture.
