Talon VTOL: 2.5 m tilt-rotor aircraft

Design stage · CAD in Python · 2026

Designed from my own sketches: a 3D-printable tricopter with 30 printable parts. A sizing tool with 50 unit tests gives a take-off mass of 5.2 kg, a stall speed of 11.4 m/s and a hover thrust margin of 2.1 times. It is not built or flown yet. Printing and a hover test come next.

CalcOS: a calculator on a custom circuit board

ESP32-S3 · LVGL · PCB · 2026

A calculator with its own operating system. I measured the parts, designed the case and keys, and stopped the PCB order until every part's position was checked. The check found a mounting error that would have wired a power pin to ground. I fixed it first, and the assembled board works.
